Grass Cutting Simulator: Unity Game Template

Grass Cutting Simulator is a complete, ready-to-play Unity game template. Steer a spinning blade across the field: every patch of grass you cut makes the blade grow, and a bigger blade can slice through fruit and even trees. Too small? The game tells you to grow first.

Each of the 18 levels has its own field with flowers, patterns and fences. Speed and time boosts help you beat the clock, and a fast finish earns three stars and a treasure chest.
